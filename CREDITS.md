# Credits

**Macbeth** is a game by **Divan van den Berg**, brought to life through a
collective approach, bringing together coding, design and AI.

## The play

*The Tragedy of Macbeth* by William Shakespeare (1564–1616). All credit for the
original play and its words belongs to him.

The text is the public-domain Moby Shakespeare, with XML markup by Jon Bosak
(1999), as distributed with the NLTK corpora. It has been converted to modern
typography (curly quotes and dashes) for the game.

## Disclaimer

The game aims to stay true to the original play, but some placements, names,
characters' appearances and events differ from it, because this is also an
interactive online game.
